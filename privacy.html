Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This is a private application used only by its owner. It connects only to Google accounts belonging to the owner, with the owner's consent.

Data it uses

It uses only the Google data covered by the permissions the owner grants on Google's sign-in screen, and only for the owner's own use. The data is processed and stored on systems the owner controls.

Sharing

Google data is not sold, and is not used for advertising or to train models. It leaves the owner's systems only in these cases:

  • content that the owner has approved is sent to its recipients;
  • encrypted data is stored in the owner's own Google account;
  • a third-party tool the owner uses for maintenance can see excerpts of the data while doing so.

Keeping and deleting data

The owner controls all stored data and can delete it at any time. Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account's security settings.
